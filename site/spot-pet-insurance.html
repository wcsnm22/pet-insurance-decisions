Spot pet insurance review, cost, quote and promo code — FAQ

How much does Spot pet insurance cost?

Spot advertises dog insurance starting from $15/month and cat insurance starting from $9/month. The dog figure assumes an accident and illness plan with 80% reimbursement, a $500 annual deductible and a $2,500 annual limit for a 2-year-old small mixed dog in ZIP 32009; the cat figure assumes 80% reimbursement, a $750 deductible and a $2,500 annual limit for a 2-year-old mixed cat in ZIP 33801. Plan costs vary by coverage, breed, age and location.

Source: https://spotpet.com/ · checked 2026-09-25

How do I get a Spot pet insurance quote?

Spot's official page has a 'Click For Price' button that opens its quote flow at spotpet.com/quote. The site FAQ says to see your price you get a free quote, because cost depends on coverage, age, breed and location.

Source: https://spotpet.com/ · checked 2026-09-25

What does Spot pet insurance cover?

Accident-only and accident and illness plans are offered, plus an optional preventive care add-on. The accident and illness plan covers dental illnesses, hereditary and breed-specific conditions, prescription foods when prescribed for an eligible accident or illness, behavioral modification, microchip implantation and a 24/7 pet health helpline. Pre-existing conditions are not covered.

Source: https://spotpet.com/ · checked 2026-09-25

What are Spot pet insurance waiting periods?

Spot plans typically have a 14-day waiting period; state-specific waiting periods vary and are listed in the sample policy. The preventative care add-on has no waiting period - its benefits start the next day.

Source: https://spotpet.com/sample-policy · checked 2026-09-25

Are there Spot pet insurance promo codes or discounts?

Spot's official sitemaps contain no promo code page (checked 2026-09-25). Discounts published on its own site: 10% off for every pet enrolled after the first, an advertised 20% employee discount through employer benefits (up to 10% group discount plus up to 10% multi-pet discount on 2nd+ pets, varies by state, not available in all states), and Spot Perks with $2,500+ in brand discounts.

Source: https://spotpet.com/cat-insurance · checked 2026-09-25

Spot displays 4.7 / 5 based on 8,300+ reviews on its homepage. That is the company's own displayed figure.
