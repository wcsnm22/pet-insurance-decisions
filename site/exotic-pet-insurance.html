Exotic pet insurance 2026: who covers birds, rabbits and reptiles, read from each brand's own pages on 2026-09-30

Quick answer: of the four brands checked, only Nationwide sells exotic pet insurance. Its own exotic page says it is 'the only pet insurer to cover birds and exotic pets', lists six groups it covers (birds, rabbits, mini pigs & goats, lizards/reptiles & frogs, guinea pigs, ferrets & small mammals) and prices cover at 'generally starts at less than $21/mo', with quotes on 844-397-8937 - while stating that wellness coverage is not available for exotic pets at this time. The three comparison brands do not sell it: GEICO partners with Embrace 'to help you get pet insurance coverage for your dogs and cats', Allstate names exotic cover as a separate product type and then sends its quote button to Embrace for cats and dogs, and ASPCA's plan menu runs from puppy to senior dog with no exotic tier. Nationwide's exotic exclusions, in its own words

Because Nationwide is the only seller here, its exclusions are the practical rules for anyone with a bird, rabbit or reptile. From the 'Which exotic pets are not covered?' answer read on 2026-09-30, the not-covered list is:

  • venomous or poisonous species in the genus groups it lists;
  • endangered or threatened species;
  • any listed species that needs a permit, licence or registration by state or federal law;
  • any listed species that is illegal to own by state or federal law;
  • hybrids of domesticated pets with a wild/non-domesticated species;
  • species that are kept in flocks or on display; and
  • any other species not listed.

That last line is the one to read twice: if the animal is not on Nationwide's list, there is no exotic cover to buy - which is different from the three brands that simply have no exotic product at all.

Exotic pet insurance - FAQ

Does any pet insurance cover exotic pets?

Of the four brands read on 2026-09-30, only Nationwide does: its own page says it is 'the only pet insurer to cover birds and exotic pets', lists six exotic groups and prices the plan at 'generally starts at less than $21/mo'. GEICO and Allstate sell dog and cat cover through Embrace, and ASPCA's plan menu lists only dog and cat products.

Source: https://www.petinsurance.com/exotics/ · checked 2026-09-30

Which animals count as an exotic pet at Nationwide?

Its page lists six groups: Birds, Rabbits, Mini pigs & goats, Lizards, reptiles & frogs, Guinea pigs, and Ferrets & small mammals - and defines 'exotic' as an umbrella term covering small mammals, birds, reptiles and amphibians.

Source: https://www.petinsurance.com/exotics/ · checked 2026-09-30

How much does exotic pet insurance cost?

Nationwide prints one figure on its exotic page: premiums 'generally starts at less than $21/mo', varying by type of pet and amount of coverage. No deductible, reimbursement percentage or annual limit is published there - it points to policy documents instead - and quotes come via 844-397-8937.

Source: https://www.petinsurance.com/exotics/ · checked 2026-09-30

What exotic pets does Nationwide NOT cover?

Its published exclusions: venomous or poisonous species in the listed genus groups, endangered or threatened species, species that need a state or federal permit/licence/registration, species illegal to own, hybrids of domesticated pets with wild species, species kept in flocks or on display, and any species not on its list.

Source: https://www.petinsurance.com/exotics/ · checked 2026-09-30

Is wellness or routine care covered for an exotic pet?

No - Nationwide's exotic page answers directly: 'Wellness coverage is not available for exotic pets at this time.' Routine care for birds, rabbits and reptiles therefore has to be budgeted separately.

Source: https://www.petinsurance.com/exotics/ · checked 2026-09-30

Does GEICO cover birds and reptiles?

Its own page says the GEICO Insurance Agency partnered with Embrace 'to help you get pet insurance coverage for your dogs and cats' - no exotic product appears on the page read 2026-09-30. GEICO does publish what exotic pets cost to own (birds ~$295 plus $185+ a year), but never attaches cover to them.

Source: https://www.geico.com/pet-insurance/ · checked 2026-09-30

Does Allstate cover exotic pets?

Allstate's page names the category - 'Birds, reptiles, and other non-traditional pets usually require a type of coverage called exotic pet insurance' - but its own products are cat and dog insurance sold through Embrace, and its quote button leaves allstate.com for the Embrace site.

Source: https://www.allstate.com/pet-insurance · checked 2026-09-30

Does ASPCA Pet Health Insurance cover exotic pets?

Nothing exotic is offered on the official site: the plan menu read on 2026-09-30 lists Dog, Cat, Puppy, Kitten, Senior Dog and Senior Cat Insurance only, and the site's claim examples are cats and dogs.

Source: https://www.aspcapetinsurance.com/ · checked 2026-09-30

How much does it cost to own an exotic pet in the first year?

GEICO's own cost guide prints these starting-plus-annual figures: hermit crabs ~$80 then $180+ a year, gerbils and hamsters ~$180 then $150+, fish ~$160 then $520, leopard gecko ~$149+ then $290+, and birds ~$295 then $185+ - against cats ~$1,200 then $800+ and dogs ~$1,500+ then $1,500+.

Source: https://www.geico.com/living/how-much-does-it-cost-to-own-a-pet/ · checked 2026-09-30

Can I use any veterinarian for an exotic pet?

Nationwide's exotic page says its plan covers medical services and treatment from any licensed veterinarian, and that you are free to use specialists and emergency providers - the same freedom its dog and cat policies describe.

Source: https://www.petinsurance.com/exotics/ · checked 2026-09-30

Why do the three comparison brands have no exotic price?

Because none of them sells an exotic plan: GEICO and Allstate route quotes to Embrace for dogs and cats, and ASPCA's menu has no exotic tier. With no product, there is no official price to publish - so those cells are recorded as not published rather than filled in.

Source: https://www.geico.com/pet-insurance/ · checked 2026-09-30
