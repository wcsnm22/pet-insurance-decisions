FAQ

What is pet insurance?

Pet insurance reimburses you for the cost of covered vet bills. You take your pet to the vet and pay the bill, then submit a claim and get reimbursement after the claim is processed. Which treatments are covered, the deductible, the reimbursement percentage and the annual limit all depend on the plan you choose and the brand's policy terms.

Source: https://spotpet.com/ · checked 2026-09-25

How much does pet insurance cost?

On the brands' own published figures: Lemonade starts at $10/month (basic accident and illness) and averages around $48/month for dogs and $27/month for cats; Spot advertises from $15/month for dogs and $9/month for cats under specific plan settings; Fetch averages $35/month for dogs and $22/month for cats for a $10,000 annual limit, $300 deductible and 80% reimbursement over 07.01.2024-06.30.2025. Your price depends on your pet's age, breed, your location and the coverage you pick.

Source: https://www.fetchpet.com/pet-insurance-cost · checked 2026-09-25

How long are pet insurance waiting periods?

Lemonade: 14 days for illnesses and 30 days for orthopedic conditions. Spot: typically 14 days, with state-specific variations listed in its sample policy. Fetch: up to 15 days for accident and illness coverage.

Source: https://www.lemonade.com/pet-insurance · checked 2026-09-25

Does pet insurance cover pre-existing conditions?

No. All three brands state that pre-existing conditions are not covered. Spot adds that a curable condition which has been symptom-free and treatment-free for 180 days is no longer considered pre-existing and can become eligible.

Source: https://spotpet.com/ · checked 2026-09-25

Are there legitimate pet insurance promo codes?

None of the three brands publishes a promo code page in its official sitemap (checked 2026-09-25). The discounts that exist on their own sites are: Lemonade - multi-pet, bundle and annual-payment discounts; Spot - 10% off for each pet after the first, an advertised 20% employee discount via employer benefits (composed of up to 10% group plus up to 10% multi-pet on 2nd+ pets, varies by state) and Spot Perks brand discounts; Fetch - 10% off for veterans and active military (maximum available discount is 10%) and Fetch Deals member savings.

Source: https://www.fetchpet.com/faqs/military-pet-insurance-discount · checked 2026-09-25

Which vets can I use with pet insurance?

All three brands state you can use any licensed vet: Lemonade says any licensed vet in the United States, Spot says any licensed vet in the United States or Canada, Fetch says any veterinarian in the U.S. or Canada including specialists and emergency clinics.

Source: https://www.fetchpet.com/ · checked 2026-09-25
