What each brand actually offers

Lemonade runs the broadest menu: up to 10% multi-pet applied automatically, up to 5% for paying annually at renewal (plus the monthly $1 transaction fee waived) and up to 10% for bundling other Lemonade policies - but all three apply only to the base accident and illness policy plus the vet visit fee and physical therapy add-ons, never to Preventative Care packages or other add-ons. New customers also get a Rover gift card that unlocks 2 months after the policy starts, one per account.

Spot keeps it simpler: 10% off every pet after the first, up to 20% for employees of participating employers (10% group + 10% multi-pet, state-limited), and Spot Perks - third-party member promo codes that its own terms say are not insurance. Spot also publishes a Pet Supermarket dog-wash offer, again a perk rather than a premium discount.

Fetch focuses on membership offers: 10% every month for life for veterans, active military and AARP members, 10% every month for a whole year for Walmart shoppers, plus Fetch Deals (over $1,500 in savings, $40+ average per order) inside every policy. Fetch caps everything at a maximum of 10% that is not valid with any other offer.

FAQ

Is there a valid pet insurance promo code for Lemonade, Spot or Fetch?

Not on their own sites as of 2026-09-25: none of the three official sitemaps lists a promo-code or coupon page for the policy. The official discounts that do exist are tabled above - multi-pet, annual and bundle for Lemonade, multi-pet and employer for Spot, military, AARP, Walmart and Fetch Deals for Fetch. Any code sold on a third-party coupon site cannot be verified against these official pages, so this guide does not repeat them.

Source: https://www.lemonade.com/sitemap.xml · checked 2026-09-25

What is the biggest official pet insurance discount?

Spot's employer program: up to 20% off, made of up to 10% group discount plus up to 10% multi-pet discount on the 2nd and later pets - with the footnote that it varies by state, is subject to change and is not available in all states. Fetch caps every discount at a maximum of 10% and states it is not valid with any other offer. Lemonade's stack tops out at up to 10% multi-pet, up to 10% bundle and up to 5% for annual payment.

Source: https://spotpet.com/employers · checked 2026-09-25

Do insurance discounts apply to wellness plans and add-ons?

For Lemonade, no: all available Lemonade Pet discounts (multi-pet, bundle, annual payment) apply to the price of the base accident and illness policy plus the vet visit fee and physical therapy add-ons, while Preventative Care packages and other add-ons get no discount. Spot and Fetch do not publish a matching statement about add-ons on their official pages, so for those two it is not published on the official site.

Source: https://www.lemonade.com/pet/insurance-guide/lemonade-pet-renewals/ · checked 2026-09-25

How do I get the multi-pet discount?

Lemonade applies up to 10% automatically when more than one canine or feline is signed up for Lemonade Pet. Spot applies 10% off each additional pet - the 2nd pet and every pet enrolled after that - and shows more information at checkout. Fetch does not publish a multi-pet discount on its official pages.

Source: https://www.lemonade.com/pet/insurance-guide/lemonade-pet-renewals/ · checked 2026-09-25

Are these discounts available in every state?

No, and all three say so: Lemonade states its pet discounts might not be available in every state, Spot's footnotes say discounts vary by state, are subject to change and are not available in all states, and Fetch states its maximum 10% discount may not be available in all states. Your final price is confirmed in each brand's own quote flow.

Source: https://www.lemonade.com/pet/insurance-guide/lemonade-pet-renewals/ · checked 2026-09-25

What is Spot Perks - is it a discount on my premium?

No. Spot Perks is member access to promo codes from third-party pet brands, and its terms state that Spot Perks are not insurance. The terms also note that the code may be unique to Spot even when the same promotion is available directly from the merchant, so it pays to compare before you buy.

Source: https://spotpet.com/perks-terms · checked 2026-09-25

What is Fetch Deals?

Fetch Deals is included in every Fetch policy at no extra cost and is available on day one: the official page advertises over $1,500 in savings and an average of $40+ per order on pet food, grooming products, litter and more. Fetch's own terms note that Fetch Deals is not insurance and is not provided by AXIS Insurance Company.

Source: https://www.fetchpet.com/deals · checked 2026-09-25
