Lemonade vs Spot pet insurance: the published facts, side by side

Quick answer: on the numbers the two brands put on their own sites, Lemonade publishes the lower dog entry price ($10/month against Spot's $15/month) and says it pays 50% of eligible claims instantly through AI; Spot publishes the lower cat entry price ($9/month), a published full-claim turnaround of 48 hours or less, no upper age limit and a 30-day money-back guarantee. Neither publishes the same set of figures, so this page does not crown a winner - it shows what each one actually publishes, with the condition attached to every number.

How this comparison is built

There is no neutral score for Lemonade vs Spot. What this page does instead is put the figures each company publishes on its own website side by side, keep the conditions attached to every figure, and link to the page it came from. No figure here is estimated, scored or taken from a third-party comparison site, and where an official page does not publish something, the cell says not published on the official site.

All figures were read from lemonade.com and spotpet.com on 2026-09-25. Spot's waiting-period wording comes from the sample Accident & Illness policy PDF that spotpet.com/sample-policy links to (California form PET-P-20000-CA-IAIC-1024); state forms differ.

The two brands also publish different kinds of numbers: Lemonade publishes averages and state tables, Spot publishes claim turnaround and guarantee windows. A blank cell in the table below usually means the other brand simply publishes that category of figure and this one does not.

Where each one wins on the published facts

Lemonade, if you want the lower published dog price and instant partial payment. Its cost guide lists policies starting at $10/month, averages of about $48/month for dogs and about $27/month for cats, five deductible options ($100 to $1,000), annual limits from $5,000 to $100,000, the statement that adjusting coverage levels might lower the premium by 10% or 15%, and the claim that 50% of eligible claims are paid instantly through AI. Source: lemonade.com cost guide and lemonade.com/pet-insurance, checked 2026-09-25.

Spot, if published claim speed, age terms and a refund window matter most. It publishes claims reimbursed in 48 hours or less with most processed in under 2 business days (its own admin data 2019-2024), a sample claim of $11,110 back on a $12,345 bill, no upper age limit and no vet exam to enrol, a 30-day money-back guarantee (15 days in ME, LA and WA; not available in NY), a 10% multi-pet discount on every pet after the first, and a preventative add-on whose benefits start the next day. Source: spotpet.com, spotpet.com/sample-policy and spotpet.com/dog-insurance, checked 2026-09-25.

How to choose without a fake ranking

Run the brands' own test against each other: take one configuration - a $500 deductible, an 80% reimbursement rate and a $2,500 annual limit - and request the same quote for the same pet at lemonade.com and spotpet.com. Then compare those two quotes with each other, not with the advertised figures above, because advertised figures carry conditions (Spot's dog price assumes a specific 2-year-old dog in ZIP 32009).

Three checks that change the outcome more than the brand name: (1) the waiting period before coverage starts - 14 days for illnesses at Lemonade and 14 days for illness in Spot's sample policy, with different orthopedic wording; (2) whether a condition your pet already has counts as pre-existing - neither brand covers them, but Spot publishes a 180-day symptom- and treatment-free rule for curable conditions that Lemonade does not; (3) whether you want wellness care bundled in - Lemonade prices preventative packages from +$10/month and Spot prices its Platinum cat add-on at $24.95/month with a $450 annual benefit.

Lemonade vs Spot FAQ

Is Lemonade or Spot cheaper?

On published entry prices, Lemonade is lower for dogs ($10/month against Spot's $15/month) and Spot is lower for cats ($9/month against Lemonade's $10/month). The published averages tell a different story for dogs only: Lemonade says its policyholders pay about $48/month for dogs and about $27/month for cats, while Spot publishes no average of its own. Because Spot's advertised prices carry a $500 or $750 deductible and a $2,500 annual limit for specific pets in specific ZIP codes, the only fair comparison is two quotes for your own pet on the two official sites.

Source: https://www.lemonade.com/pet/insurance-guide/pet-insurance-cost/ · checked 2026-09-25

Which one pays claims faster?

Both publish a speed figure, measured differently. Lemonade says it pays 50% of eligible claims instantly through AI and pays approved claims into the bank details you enter in the app. Spot says claims are reimbursed in 48 hours or less, with most processed in under 2 business days, based on its own admin data from 2019 to 2024. Neither figure is an independent measurement, and payment still depends on the claim being covered by the policy.

Source: https://spotpet.com/ · checked 2026-09-25

What are the waiting periods?

Lemonade states 14 days for illnesses and 30 days for orthopedic conditions, with preventative package benefits usable the day after purchase. Spot's sample Accident & Illness policy states a 14-day waiting period for illness including congenital, hereditary and orthopedic illness, starting on the first effective date of coverage, and says a paid Waiting Period Health Assessment can modify it; Spot's preventative care coverage has no waiting period, so those benefits start the next day.

Source: https://www.lemonade.com/pet-insurance · checked 2026-09-25

Do they cover pre-existing conditions?

Neither does. Lemonade treats anything your pet showed signs of before the waiting periods are up as pre-existing, while covering accidents and new illnesses as base coverage. Spot excludes pre-existing conditions but reclassifies a cured and curable condition that has been symptom-free and treatment-free for 180 days, except knee and ligament injuries.

Source: https://spotpet.com/ · checked 2026-09-25

Which one has better discounts?

Lemonade publishes a multi-pet discount whose eligibility depends on where you live, a bundle discount with other Lemonade policies, and a discount for paying annually instead of monthly. Spot publishes 10% off every pet enrolled after the first, an advertised 20% employee discount through employer benefits (up to a 10% group discount plus up to a 10% multi-pet discount on 2nd+ pets, varies by state) and Spot Perks with $2,500+ in brand discounts. Neither brand publishes a promo code page in its official sitemap, so a code you find on a third-party site is not something either brand publishes.

Source: https://www.lemonade.com/pet/insurance-guide/pet-insurance-cost/ · checked 2026-09-25

Can I use my own vet with either brand?

Yes in both cases. Lemonade says you can go to any vet in the United States as long as they are licensed to provide vet care in their state, and Spot says you can visit any licensed vet in the United States or Canada. Both reimburse you rather than paying the vet directly, so you settle the bill and submit the claim.

Source: https://www.lemonade.com/pet-insurance · checked 2026-09-25

Which is better for an older pet?

Spot is the one that publishes an age policy: it covers puppies and kittens from 8 weeks old, states there is no upper age limit and no vet exam to enrol, and also states that premiums are based on your pet's current age and may increase at renewal. Lemonade does not publish a minimum or maximum enrolment age on the pages read here, so its age terms are not published on the official site.

Source: https://spotpet.com/ · checked 2026-09-25
