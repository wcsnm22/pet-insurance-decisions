Fetch pet insurance review, cost, quote and promo code — FAQ

How much does Fetch pet insurance cost?

Fetch publishes averages of $35/month for dogs and $22/month for cats, based on U.S. annual premiums with the most common configuration of a $10,000 annual limit, $300 deductible and 80% reimbursement rate for 07.01.2024 through 06.30.2025. Its breed averages run from $24/month (Domestic Shorthair cats) to $71/month (French Bulldogs). Your price varies by pet age, breed and where you live.

Source: https://www.fetchpet.com/pet-insurance-cost · checked 2026-09-25

How do I get a Fetch pet insurance quote?

Fetch's quote flow starts from the 'Get your price' button on its official site, which opens its pet setup page at fetchpet.com/mypet. Fetch states your quote includes all monthly taxes and fees.

Source: https://www.fetchpet.com/ · checked 2026-09-25

What does Fetch pet insurance cover?

Accident and illness coverage with up to 90% reimbursement, exam fees up to $250 per sick visit, every adult tooth (not just canines), online vet visits up to $1,000 per year at no extra cost, and 100% reimbursement on qualifying pet meds through Fetch Pet Rx (subject to deductible and policy limit). A Wellness add-on covers routine and preventive care. Pre-existing conditions are not covered.

Source: https://www.fetchpet.com/ · checked 2026-09-25

What are Fetch pet insurance waiting periods?

The waiting period for accident and illness coverage with Fetch is up to 15 days. Fetch emails you as soon as your coverage starts.

Source: https://www.fetchpet.com/ · checked 2026-09-25

Are there Fetch pet insurance promo codes or discounts?

Fetch's official sitemap contains no promo code page (checked 2026-09-25). Discounts published on its own site: veterans and active military save 10% every month for life (maximum available discount is 10%, subject to approval, may not be available in all states, not valid with any other offer), and Fetch Deals member savings of $1,500+ included at no extra cost.

Source: https://www.fetchpet.com/faqs/military-pet-insurance-discount · checked 2026-09-25

What do reviews of Fetch pet insurance say on its own site?

Fetch's homepage says thousands of 5-star reviews on Trustpilot and labels itself '#1 Pet Insurance, Readers' Choice 2026'. Those are the company's own claims as displayed on its site.

Source: https://www.fetchpet.com/ · checked 2026-09-25
