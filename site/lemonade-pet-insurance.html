Lemonade pet insurance review, cost, quote and promo code — FAQ

How much does Lemonade pet insurance cost?

Lemonade states that policies for dogs and cats start at $10/month for basic accident and illness coverage. Its own averages are around $48/month for dogs and around $27/month for cats; its example quotes are $61/month for a 4-year-old Goldendoodle in Chicago and $29/month for a 1-year-old short-haired cat in Danbury, Connecticut, both at $250 deductible and 80% co-insurance. Cost varies by coverage, breed, age and location.

Source: https://www.lemonade.com/pet/insurance-guide/pet-insurance-cost/ · checked 2026-09-25

How do I get a Lemonade pet insurance quote?

Lemonade's own site says a quote takes as little as 90 seconds. The quote entry point on its pet insurance page is labeled 'CHECK OUR PRICES'.

Source: https://www.lemonade.com/pet/insurance-guide/pet-insurance-cost/ · checked 2026-09-25

What does Lemonade pet insurance cover?

The official page lists bloodwork, X-rays, MRI, CT scans, lab work, ultrasounds, cancer, emergency care, surgery, heart disease, hospitalization, road accidents, outpatient care, allergy medicine, specialty care, injections, prescription medications, arthritis medication and knee/elbow/hip dysplasia. Vet exam fees are covered only with the Visit Fee Add-on and only for a covered accident or illness. Coverage is subject to policy terms.

Source: https://www.lemonade.com/pet-insurance · checked 2026-09-25

What are Lemonade pet insurance waiting periods?

14 days for illnesses and 30 days for orthopedic conditions. If you bought a preventative package, its benefits can be used the day after purchase.

Source: https://www.lemonade.com/pet-insurance · checked 2026-09-25

Are there Lemonade pet insurance promo codes or discounts?

Lemonade's official sitemap contains no promo code or coupon page (checked 2026-09-25). The discounts published on its own site are a multi-pet discount (eligibility depends on where you live), a bundle discount with other Lemonade policies, and a discount for paying annually instead of monthly.

Source: https://www.lemonade.com/pet/insurance-guide/pet-insurance-cost/ · checked 2026-09-25

What do reviews of Lemonade pet insurance say on its own site?

Lemonade reports 4.9 stars in the App Store and on Google Play on its pet insurance page. That is Lemonade's self-reported figure, not an independent audit.

Source: https://www.lemonade.com/pet-insurance · checked 2026-09-25
